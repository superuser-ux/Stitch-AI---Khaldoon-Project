**carousel.deep.01 — Final Framework Guidelines**

Deep Transformation Carousel \| 7 Slides + Closing Sections

**PURPOSE**

A multi-slide carousel designed to create cognitive disruption and shift
belief through layered truth-telling. Each slide builds on the last —
from provoking awareness, to anchoring insight, to delivering a concrete
behavioural shift. The reader should finish feeling seen, challenged,
and equipped.

**CORE PRINCIPLES**

- One core belief to challenge per carousel — no topic mixing

- Pain → Awareness → Reframe → Ownership → Action

- Every slide earns the next — no filler slides

- Truth is more powerful than comfort — don't soften the message

- Specificity over generality — real scenarios, not abstract concepts

- Closure must feel earned, not wrapped in positivity

**STRUCTURE (LOCKED — 7 Slides)**

**Slide 1 — Hook** \| الصدمة الأولى — Reality Trigger

> One sharp, provocative statement that challenges a common belief. Must
> create instant recognition ('that's me') or instant discomfort. No
> intro, no context. Hit immediately.

**Slide 2 — Concept Anchor** \| ربط المفهوم — The Core Frame

> Name and define the hidden mechanism causing the problem. Use a
> memorable label or analogy (e.g. 'The Museum Trap'). This is the
> intellectual backbone of the carousel.

**Slide 3 — Human Proof** \| أدلة بشرية — Real-Life Snapshots

> 2–3 micro-portraits of real people experiencing the problem. Short,
> specific, recognisable. Each snapshot = one archetype. Reader should
> see themselves or someone they know in at least one.

**Slide 4 — Distillation** \| الحقيقة المجردة — The Cold Truth

> Break the truth into 3 lenses: psychological / behavioural /
> practical. This is the deepest slide — no softening. Each lens = one
> sentence, sharp and clear.

**Slide 5 — Practical Bridge** \| جسر التحول — The Shift

> Reframe the question the reader has been asking (wrong question →
> right question). Then give the concrete behavioural shift. Must be
> actionable, not motivational.

**Slide 6 — Meaningful Closure** \| الإغلاق الحقيقي — The Landing

> A single powerful line that redefines the original topic at a deeper
> level. Not a summary — a transformation of the opening belief. The
> reader should feel the circle has closed.

**Slide 7 — CTA** \| دعوة للتأمل — Reflective Prompt

> A question that invites honest self-reflection, not surface
> engagement. Should feel personal and slightly uncomfortable. Avoid
> generic CTAs like 'share if you agree'.

**CLOSING SECTIONS (For Caption / Post Context)**

These appear in the caption or as supplementary context, not inside the
slides:

**The Positive Side**

> A one-line reframe that acknowledges healthy versions of the pattern
> being challenged. Prevents the post from reading as purely critical.

**Science**

> One psychological or neuroscience fact that validates the carousel's
> core claim. Accessible language only — no jargon. Should strengthen,
> not replace, the emotional logic.

**DO**

- Use Palestinian dialect for spoken/caption text

- Make Slide 1 feel like an accusation the reader can't ignore

- Use a named analogy or concept label in Slide 2 (e.g. 'The Glass Child
  Trap')

- Ground Slide 3 in archetypes the audience recognises from real life

- In Slide 4, speak in three distinct lenses — one sentence each

- End with a question that costs the reader something to answer honestly

- Keep each slide to one idea — one sentence if possible on key slides

**DON'T**

- Mix two problems or two beliefs in one carousel

- Add comfort before the truth has landed

- Use generic CTAs ('comment below', 'save this', 'share if you agree')

- Explain too much — trust the reader to connect the dots

- Use science as decoration — only include it if it sharpens the point

- End with a motivational quote — end with a shift

- Skip the Concept Anchor — it is the intellectual spine of the carousel

**SLIDE LENGTH GUIDE**

**Slide 1 Hook** 1 sentence. Maximum 15 words. No explanation.

**Slide 2 Concept Anchor** 2–3 sentences. Name the concept. Define it
briefly.

**Slide 3 Human Proof** 2–3 micro-portraits. 1–2 sentences each.

**Slide 4 Distillation** 3 sentences — one per lens. No more.

**Slide 5 Practical Bridge** 3–4 sentences. Question reframe + action.

**Slide 6 Closure** 1–2 sentences. The most memorable line in the
carousel.

**Slide 7 CTA** 1 question. Personal and specific.

"Using carousel.deep.01 framework, write a 7-slide carousel about \[ …
\].\
One belief to challenge only. Palestinian dialect.\
Include The Positive Side and Science closing sections."
