## 1. What "building the engine" actually means
Right now, the system exists on paper and we've proven it can write in Moataz's voice. "Building" means turning that into a working tool where:

- You say *"plan the next 28 days"* and it lays out all 56 posts (right pillars, formats, struggles, lenses — no repeats).
- It writes the topic, hook, and script for each one, in Palestinian dialect, following all your rules.
- You review them in one place — a simple dashboard, or by tapping **Approve / Reject / Change** in Telegram.
- Approved scripts are ready to hand to your production/filming team.

That's Phase 1. Editing, posting, and analytics come later (Stage 3). We build the brain first.
