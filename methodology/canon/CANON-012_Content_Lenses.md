# CANON-012 — Content Lenses

## Definition
A lens is the specific relationship between the viewer and their wound. It is not a topic, format, or tone.

## The 5 Lenses
| Lens | Arabic | English | Viewer State | Primary Action | Default Hook Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | التشخيص | The Mirror | Does not know the wound has a name | Share | Painful Truth |
| L2 | الزلزال | The Earthquake | Has a wrong story about the wound | Save | The Collision |
| L3 | المفتاح | The Key | Wants one concrete action | Save | The Promise |
| L4 | القصة | The Story | Needs evidence that change is possible | Share | The Drop |
| L5 | العمق | The Deep | Has tried everything and feels defeated | Comments + DMs | Painful Truth |

## Lens Questions
### L1 — The Mirror
- What is this thing I feel?
- Is what I feel normal or is something wrong with me?
- Why do I feel it now even if nothing obvious happened?
- How does it appear in daily life without me noticing?
- What is the real name of what I carry?

### L2 — The Earthquake
- What wrong understanding was I carrying?
- Why did my old way of handling it make it worse?
- What truth has no one said this clearly before?
- How does this new understanding change everything?
- What first step follows from the correct understanding?

### L3 — The Key
- What one step makes a difference tonight?
- Why is this step the most important?
- How do I apply this tool correctly?
- What happens if I apply it consistently?
- What science supports this tool?

### L4 — The Story
- Who lived this wound and what was their state?
- What moment changed everything?
- What exactly did they do and how did they start?
- How did life change afterward in concrete terms?
- What message does this story leave for someone in the same place?

### L5 — The Deep
- What has this person tried that did not work?
- Why did it not work — what wound is under the wound?
- What has no one told this person but they need specifically?
- Is there hope for someone at this point, and why?
- What is the first step, not toward success, but toward beginning?

## Mandatory Delivery Check
Every script must contain a scroll-stopping hook, complete emotional journey, metaphor, scientific truth, Qur'an or Hadith when organic, light human moment, and unforgettable final sentence.

## Golden Formula
`42 HCS × 5 lenses = 210 content pieces per round.`

Two rounds per year create 420 pieces without repetition by changing lens and angle.
